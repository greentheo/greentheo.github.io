Ambient

live

A granular instrument for live performance. Eight voices, no timeline, always running — you sculpt it rather than arrange it.

No shared clock. Every voice sweeps its source on its own cycle, so give them 7.3s, 11.1s and 4.7s and they will drift against each other for hours without ever realigning.

Amp

live

A guitar amplifier in a browser tab. Cascaded valve preamp, push-pull power amp with sag, and a speaker cabinet. 5.3 ms round trip.

The tone stack is the real circuit. Third-order transfer function from the published analysis, component values off the schematics, and checked against the analogue response to within 0.05 dB.

Unfold to Sheets

live

Take a 3D model, cut it into pieces that flatten, nest those onto sheet stock, and mark the seams so it can be put back together.

It sweeps the scale. The interesting output is not one layout but the curve of waste against size — how big you should build it.

Lofting

live

Turn a scanned or photographed boat lines plan into a table of offsets you can scale to any size.

Calibration is the whole problem. A photo of a plan is perspective-distorted, and the accuracy of every offset depends on getting that undone first.

Caliper

source

Spec-driven generation of manufacturable 3D parts. Describe the part, get an executable spec, generate the mesh, measure it, iterate until it passes.

Test-driven development for physical parts. Wall thickness, hole inventory, mass, interference and watertightness are measured exactly and graded against the spec. The numbers are ground truth; the render is only a sanity check.

Plasma Nesting

source

2D nesting for CNC plasma cutting. Parses the cut shapes back out of G-code, packs them onto a sheet to waste less steel, and emits new G-code panels.

It reads the toolpaths you already have. Nothing to redraw and nothing to re-export — G-code in, packed G-code out.

The Firm

source

An operating model for running several coding agents on one real codebase. Five roles, one owner, a labelled board, and seven rules.

One agent in one checkout is a solved problem. The trouble starts at two — branches switched out from under each other, authority arriving sideways from a peer, and “tests pass” from a session that read it rather than ran it.

Cartograph

source

A visual map of any repository, built from the source rather than from a model’s impression of it, with coding agents attached at every level.

A map that can be wrong in a way you notice. The crawler extracts what the code actually says — modules, imports, resolved call edges — and every element carries a file:line you can go and check.
